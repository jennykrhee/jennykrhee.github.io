Teaching

My teaching focuses on helping students become independent problem solvers with technology and analytics. I combine conceptual understanding with hands-on practice so that students learn not only how to use analytical tools, but when and why to use them. As AI becomes increasingly integrated into analytical work, I also encourage students to use AI to support their reasoning rather than substitute for it.
